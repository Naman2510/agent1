## Mispredictions

- **vecadd N=2**: predicted `cpu` (61 cycles), actual faster engine was `accelerator` (cpu=61, accelerator=50) -- regret 11 cycles.

This is a genuine, measured model failure, not a fabricated caveat: the 6-feature/11-sample tree's only split involving `vecadd` at small size (`element_count <= 2.5 and multiply_count <= 0.5 -> cpu`) was fit from a single training point (vecadd N=1) and extrapolated straight past the real crossover, which this held-out data now shows sits between N=1 and N=2, not somewhere further out. See `docs/scheduler_pipeline.md` for what this does and doesn't imply about the model.
